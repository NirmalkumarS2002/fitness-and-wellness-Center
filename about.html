Our Story

From one small room to a wellness home

Vitalyn started with a simple idea: fitness should feel welcoming, not intimidating. We began with a few weights and a lot of patience.

Today we bring strength training, yoga, nutrition and recovery together, so you can look after your whole self in one place.

Our Journey

Twelve years of growing together

2014

A small training room

Vitalyn opened with two coaches and a handful of free weights in Anna Nagar.

2017

Yoga and nutrition join in

We added mindful movement classes and our first registered dietitian.

2021

The recovery lounge opens

Steam, stretch zone and massage chairs made recovery part of every plan.

2024

Moving into a full studio

A larger space with three training floors, a juice bar and 40+ weekly classes.

2026

Thousands of members strong

Our community keeps growing, and so does our team of certified coaches.
