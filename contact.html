Is the first week really free?add

Yes. You can attend any group class and book one personal training session during your trial week at no cost.

What should I bring?add

Comfortable workout clothes, shoes, a water bottle and a small towel. Lockers and a shower area are available.

I am a complete beginner. Is that okay?add

Absolutely. Every class has beginner options, and your coach will build a plan around your current level.

Do you offer nutrition guidance?add

Our registered dietitians give simple, realistic meal plans. You can add nutrition coaching to any membership.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?add

You can pause your membership for travel or health reasons, and cancel any time with 15 days notice.
